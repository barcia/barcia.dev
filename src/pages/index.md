Digital product builder. I take products from idea to production, with a special craft for user interfaces and AI in the loop. Based in Galicia, Spain, working remotely.

## How can I help you

I've been building for the web for more than 8 years. My background is frontend and UI: React, Next.js, Astro and TypeScript, design systems and plenty of Shopify. I also trained in interaction design, so I care as much about how a product feels as about how it's built.

For the last two years I've worked on teams at the forefront of AI, building AI products for banks, insurers and businesses, and building them the AI-native way, with agents as part of the team before it became the norm. That changed how I work. Today I work across the whole product, from design to frontend and backend (Node and the JavaScript stack), and ship at a pace that wasn't possible before.

Here's where I can help:

- **Join your product team** as a product engineer, full-time or part-time.
- **Build your product** from the first idea to production: the interface, the frontend and what's behind it.
- **Take your UI further:** design systems, complex products made simple, and closing the gap between design and code.
- **Bring AI into how you work:** designing and implementing AI workflows, from agentic development setups for engineering teams (harnesses, agents, MCP and skills) to MCP servers and integrations that connect AI to your tools and data.
- **Online stores on Shopify,** like the ones I built for FC Barcelona and Levante UD.

I like software that's simple and well made: clear interfaces, solid foundations and nothing it doesn't need.

Whether it's a role, a project or a hand bringing AI into your team, let's talk.
